# Voice Profile (Tight v2)

## Positioning
Senior analytics + BI leader writing for operators.

## Tone
- Direct, practical, slightly contrarian
- Opinion-first, evidence-backed
- No fluff, no motivational filler

## Writing rules
1. Open with a sharp POV in line 1.
2. Anchor with one concrete business consequence.
3. Give a simple framework/checklist.
4. End with one clear CTA question.
5. Keep to 130–220 words.

## Style constraints
- Avoid jargon stacking.
- Avoid fake precision or invented stats.
- Prefer short paragraphs (1–3 lines).
- Use plain English over consultant-speak.

## Signature themes
- Analytics as decision infrastructure, not reporting
- BI adoption and trust over dashboard volume
- Metrics quality/governance as GTM advantage
- AI in analytics with ROI discipline
